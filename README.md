# TBoss HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Title
TBoss HR Analytics – Executive Overview & Compensation Deep Dive

## Project Objectives
- To analyze the complete workforce profile of TBoss across demographics, departments, and business units.
- To uncover trends in hiring, tenure, salary distribution, and increment patterns.
- To build interactive dashboards that give leadership a clear, data-driven view of the organization’s human capital.
- To identify gaps and opportunities that support better talent management, compensation decisions, and diversity initiatives.

## Dataset Used
Dataset: TBoss HR Employee Records (fictional / sample dataset)  
Contains employee-level data on demographics, salary, tenure, department, business unit, country, ethnicity, and salary increments.

## Key Questions Explored
1. What is the overall size and demographic composition of the workforce (gender, age, ethnicity, country)?
2. Which departments and business units have the highest headcount?
3. How has hiring grown between 2020 and 2023?
4. What is the average and median salary across the organization?
5. How do salaries differ by department, gender, and ethnicity?
6. What percentage of employees received a salary increment, and which departments received the highest average increments?
7. Who are the top earners in the company?
8. How does average tenure vary across age groups?

## Process
1. **Data Cleaning and Preparation**
   - Standardized department, business unit, and country names.
   - Handled missing values and ensured consistent formatting of salary and tenure fields.
   - Created calculated fields for salary bands, age groups, and increment flags.

2. **Data Analysis**
   - Aggregated headcount, average/median salary, and tenure by key dimensions.
   - Analyzed year-over-year hiring trends.
   - Examined salary and increment patterns across gender, ethnicity, and department.

## Dashboard Building
- Built two interactive pages using **Google Looker Studio**.
- Page 1: **Executive Overview** – high-level workforce snapshot with KPIs, demographic composition, headcount by department/business unit, and hiring & tenure trends.
- Page 2: **Compensation & Talent Deep Dive** – detailed salary analysis, increment insights, top earners, and an employee explorer table.
- Applied consistent filters (Department, Business Unit, Country, Gender, Ethnicity, Age Group) across both pages for seamless exploration.

## Recommendations and Insights
1. **Gender Balance**  
   The workforce is nearly balanced (51.8% Female, 48.2% Male).  
   ➔ Maintain this balance while monitoring leadership and senior roles for equitable representation.

2. **Geographic Concentration**  
   64.3% of employees are based in the United States, followed by China (21.8%) and Brazil (13.9%).  
   ➔ Consider regional talent strategies and local market compensation benchmarking.

3. **Hiring Growth**  
   Hiring has steadily increased from 133 in 2020 to 352 in 2023.  
   ➔ Continue monitoring onboarding capacity and retention of newer hires.

4. **Salary & Increment Patterns**  
   Average salary is $113K (median $97K). Only 47.5–48% of employees received increments, with Marketing, Accounting, and Finance receiving the highest average increments.  
   ➔ Review increment allocation criteria to ensure fairness across departments, especially Engineering and IT which received lower average increments.

5. **Tenure & Age**  
   Average tenure is relatively consistent across age groups (around 4.3 years).  
   ➔ Explore targeted retention programs for high-performing employees in the 25–34 age band who may be more mobile.

6. **Top Earners**  
   The highest earners are predominantly Vice Presidents.  
   ➔ Use the Employee Explorer to identify high-potential mid-level talent for succession planning.

## Conclusion
This project delivers a clear, executive-ready view of TBoss’s workforce health, compensation structure, and talent trends. The two dashboards enable leadership to quickly identify demographic patterns, monitor hiring momentum, and make more informed decisions around salary, increments, and talent development.

The interactive design allows stakeholders to filter and drill down as needed, turning raw HR data into actionable insights that support strategic people decisions.
